###############################################################################
                                 (B) CONTROLS
###############################################################################

The controls for this game are very simple, and are as follows:

A - Jump.
B - Talk/Attack(with your hair)/Run(if held).
Start - Pause/check inventory/check dances. (B toggles inventory/dances)
Select - Dance(Magic).

You can also crawl by crouching and moving left/right, and up+B lets you use
items.

                                   (E) LISTS
###############################################################################

Here are some lists of where various items in the game can be found.

-------------
Heart Holders
-------------

1. In the Scarecrow Field above a pair of pillars, you can reach it with the
   monkey.
2. In the Naga Wasteland down a ladder in plain view, use vanish cream to reach
   the heart holder.
3. In the Spider Forest about halfway, use the spider to climb up a wall and 
   avoid a slippery hill.
4. Near the right side of the Mud Bog area will be a rock that you can smash
   with the elephant.  Inside the cave beyond you can use either two float
   muffins or the harpy to get the heart holder.
5. On the right side of the Male Desert, you will need the harpy to reach it.
6. In Archer Forest use the elephant to bash a tree stump and enter the cave
   below.  Use a mint and perform any dance that you know in front of the
   clown here to get rid of him, then get the heart holder beyond.
7. In Mount Pointy fly carefully left from the Twinkle Palace and you should
   find the heart holder on a cliff.


---------
Fireflies
---------

1. Visit the waterfall area at night and it will be right below you if you
   enter from the left.  You can get it with the spider and a little effort.
2. Visit the Rain Storm area at night and head right, after jumping a few gaps
   you should see it above you.  Just jump up to it to get it, no dances are
   needed.
3. Visit the Scarecrow Field at night and it will be on the third level of a
   series of pillars and plaforms.
4. Midway through the Snail Wasteland, use the spider to reach it (at night).
5. Use the monkey to reach a firefly (at night) in the hidden waterfall area
   (you will need the spider to reach this area from either side), which can
   be reached from either the Snail Wasteland or the top right corner of the
   Waterfall area.
6. In the Naga Wasteland go left past the ladder that leads to the ladies
   desert, then use the spider to climb up the wall between the spikes.  Now
   use the monkey to get on the platform above you (at night) and grab the
   firefly.
7. In the right side of the Eagle Wasteland, use the spider and monkey (at
   night) to reach it.
8. In the left side of Mount Pointy, use the monkey/harpy (at night) to reach
   it.
9. On the right side of the Mud Bog area, use the monkey and head left to the
   first wall.  Now jump to the wall above you (at night) and you should find
   the firefly up there.
10. Use the harpy to fly up at the left end of the Ghost Forest (at night)
    for a firefly.
11. In the Ghost Forest use the monkey (at night) to jump up the wall
    slightly left of the first one you smashed with the elephant for a
    firefly.
12. Near the right side of Archer Forest (at night), on top of platform in
    plain view.


-----------
Warp Squids
-----------

[Dribble Fountain Labyrinth]

1. Halfway through the third room (platforms above water) it's on a platform
   above you, use the spider to get it.
2. In the green room with light entering it near the beginning, the room where
   you first meet the large blue enemies.  It's in plain view after you crawl
   through a short gap, near impossible to miss.
3. In the room that is shaped like an inverted "7" with a shaft leading down
   to a passage that goes left.  The passage has a few platforms over water to
   jump, the warp squid is in a hidden room on the "corner" of the inverted
   "7".  Just crawl right and you will find it.
4. In the room where you must push the single pattern stone around to the
   others far below.  You will see it on your way to the stone but you won't
   be able to get to it until you can use the monkey.
5. In the room where you run across many small gaps in human form, just
   beyond you can change to the monkey and jump up a hidden passage above the
   shaft with the snake platforms.  The passage will lead to a room with an
(…)
[Golem Mine Labyrinth]

1. In the room on the left side of the first floor (beyond spikes) of the
   elevator room.  After you go left past the enemies above you, there is a
   passage above you that leads back right to the warp squid.
2. In the same room mentioned above there is a set of spikes halfway down a
   shaft on the right side.  Use the monkey to land beside them, then use the
   elephant to break them.  Finally head right past an enemy for the warp
   squid.
3. In the room where you make the spikes fall in the lava in order to cross it.
   On the left side of this lava the warp squid is above you, use the harpy to
   reach it.
4. Destroy the spikes in the first room of the labyrinth and enter the room
   beyond.  The warp squid is hidden in a passage in the top left corner of
   this room.
5. In the elevator room, between the bottom floor and the one above it you
   should see a pir of windows in the walls.  Walk through the right one for
   the warp squid.
(…)
[Cackle Mound Labyrinth]

1. At the top of the second room, in a narrow passage between shafts.  Use the
   monkey to get up there and get it.
2. In the spike room with all the eye blocks, on a platform just to the right
   of one of the eye blocks.
3. In the room with many vines on the floor and a single eye block, ride the
   eye block ALL the way up for the warp squid.
4. After getting the spider dance and returning up the shaft in the previous
   room you can use the monkey to follow an arrow to a warp squid in the next
   room.
5. In the slide room you'll want to slide to the bottom, then use the harpy to
   fly up and right of the bottom slide for a warp squid.

(…)
[Twinkle Palace Labyrinth]

1. In the same room as warp squid #2 and #4, just use the harpy and fly up
   to the top of the room and you'll get it.
2. In the room with the first locked door, fall down the wall as the monkey
   and enter a hidden passage on your left for the warp squid.
3. Halfway up the twisting shaft with many gaps that have jumping enemies,
   you should see it on the way up and you can easily get it with the harpy.
4. In the same room as warp squid #2, just do a running jump right from the
   wall near the locked door to find it.
5. Go left from the shaft in the launcher room, in this room you'll want to
   crawl right and fall into a passage that has the warp squid.
